# Choosing Our Stack

We picked **Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind**.

## Why
- App Router fits our content-driven site
- TypeScript keeps us honest
- Tailwind lets us iterate fast

## Trade-offs
- More config surface
- Tailwind class soup (we'll extract components)
